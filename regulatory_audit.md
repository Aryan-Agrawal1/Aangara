# Regulatory Audit

## Findings
1. **Discrepancy Log Consistency**: `docs/regulatory-discrepancies.md` clearly tracks changes (e.g., Iron & Steel being DRAFT, not FINAL).
2. **UI Presentation vs Law**: The UI must clearly state that modelled shortfalls/surpluses and MRV readiness scores are *not* statutory certificates or binding obligations until verified by a regulatory body.

## Actions (Before -> After)
- **Before**: UI elements might imply finality.
- **After**: MRV Readiness already has a disclaimer (`Proprietary analytical readiness assessment — not a statutory BEE/ACVA audit certificate.`). Added tooltips to "CCTS Carbon Compliance Position" emphasizing that "Modelled Potential Surplus/Shortfall" are pre-audit estimates, subject to final Verification by Accredited Carbon Verification Agencies (ACVA).
