## Record ID: `DISC-05` ? Elevation of Iron & Steel to 8th Compliance Sector
* **Date**: 2026-08-19
* **Authority**: Ministry of Power (MoP) / BEE / MoEFCC
* **Statutory Source**: Gazette Notification G.S.R. 88(E) & Trajectory Schedule 2025-26
* **Prior Baseline**: In Phase 1 MVP documentation, 7 sectors were designated as monitored compliance sectors, with Iron & Steel listed under the draft watchlist.
* **Reconciled Reality**: Iron & Steel is formally reconciled as the **8th binding compliance sector** under CCTS with baseline year FY2023-24 and binding GEI trajectories for BF-BOF, DRI-EAF, and Pellet units.
* **Resolution**: Elevated `iron_steel` from `WATCHLIST` to `FINAL` in `data/regulatory_truth/regulatory_status.json` and added subsector target trajectories in `regulatory_targets.json`.
