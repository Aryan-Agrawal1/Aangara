# CarbonAlpha Official Regulatory Source Register

## Hierarchy of Sources
- **Tier 1 (Binding / Primary Statutory)**: India Code, e-Gazette of India, Ministry of Power, Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), MoEFCC, Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC).
- **Tier 2 (Official Institutional Information)**: Press Information Bureau (PIB), Lok Sabha / Rajya Sabha parliamentary records.
- **Tier 3 (Institutional Cross-Checks)**: World Bank Carbon Pricing Dashboard, International Carbon Action Partnership (ICAP).
- **Tier 4 (Market & Professional References)**: Industry commentaries, legal analyses (informational only; never overrides Tier 1).
